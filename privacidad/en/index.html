Privacy Notice and Terms of Use – Loyalty Card and Surveys

Your privacy and control over your data matter most to us.

1 Who collects your data, and what do we ask for?

Zenzia, located at Epigmenio González 500, Fracc. San Pablo, 76130 Santiago de Querétaro, Qro., Mexico, is responsible for processing your personal data.

This notice covers the digital loyalty card (sections 1 to 5) and our satisfaction surveys (section 6).

To get your loyalty card we don’t ask for any personal data: no name, no phone number, no email, and no sensitive or banking information.

We only store what the card needs to work: an anonymous card number, your points, stamps and rewards, and the technical identifier your phone sends us so we can update your card and send you notifications.

Legal note: Apple Wallet and Google Wallet do not collect or have access to your personal information; they act only as the digital container for your card.

2 What do we use your data for?

Primary purpose (required for the program)

  • Managing and updating the points, stamps and rewards you’ve earned.

Additional purpose (you can opt out)

  • Sending you push notifications, location alerts and promotional messages with exclusive offers straight to your phone’s screen.

Location alerts are generated by your own phone when you’re near our store; we don’t track or store your location. If you’d rather not receive promotions, follow the steps in section 3: you’ll keep earning points as usual.

3 How to turn off promotions and notifications

You’re in full control. If you want to keep earning points but don’t want promotions on your screen, you can turn off alerts at any time:

On iPhone (Apple Wallet)

  1. Open the Wallet app and tap your Zenzia card.
  2. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Pass Details (or Notifications, depending on your iOS version).
  4. Turn off Allow Notifications.

Don’t see the option? Go to Settings › Notifications › Wallet and turn off Allow Notifications (this silences all your Wallet cards).

On Android (Google Wallet)

  1. Open Google Wallet and tap your Zenzia card.
  2. Tap the three dots (…) or Pass details.
  3. Turn off notifications for this card.

Don’t see the option? In Google Wallet, tap your profile picture › Wallet settings › Manage Wallet notifications, and turn off pass and nearby pass notifications.

4 Leaving the program and your ARCO rights

If you want to leave the loyalty program and have us delete your card and your data from our system, email contacto@zenzia.mx with the subject “Baja de Tarjeta de Lealtad” (Loyalty Card Removal).

You can also simply delete the card from your Wallet app: you’ll stop receiving notifications right away. To also have us delete your data from our system, send us the email above.

In that same email you can exercise your ARCO rights under Mexican law: Access your data, Rectify it, Cancel it, or Oppose its use. Since we don’t ask for personal data, include the number shown on your card or a screenshot of it so we can identify it, along with your request. We’ll reply within 20 business days at most.

5 Card terms of use

  • The card is personal, non-transferable and free of charge.
  • Points, stamps and rewards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with third parties. It’s kept in our own system, protected by security measures, and we would only hand it over to an authority if required by law.
  • If we change this notice or the program rules, we’ll post the updated version on this same page.

6 Satisfaction surveys

Our surveys are anonymous: you don’t need an account or to sign in, and we don’t ask for your name, phone number or email. Your answers are not linked to your loyalty card.

What do we store?

  • Your answers, the date you sent them, and the language you answered in (Spanish or English).
  • A cookie: when you open a survey, your browser stores a random number for one year. Our system keeps only a code derived from that number, different for each survey, so that each person answers only once. If you block cookies, you won’t be able to answer.
  • Your IP address, only temporarily: to protect surveys from abusive submissions, we limit how many answers arrive from the same connection. We don’t store your IP in our database; only an encrypted code that deletes itself after a few minutes.

What do we use your answers for?

  • Understanding your opinion and producing statistics to improve our products and service.

We don’t use surveys to send you promotions or to build profiles. Only authorized Zenzia staff can see the results, and we don’t sell or share your answers with third parties. We keep them for as long as they’re useful for that analysis.

Our advice: don’t write personal data (name, phone number, email) in open-ended questions.

Your rights

Because answers are anonymous, we can’t tell which ones are yours. If you wrote personal data in an answer and want us to delete it, email contacto@zenzia.mx with the subject “Encuestas – Datos personales” (Surveys – Personal data), stating the survey, the approximate date and the text you wrote. We’ll reply within 20 business days at most. You can also delete the cookie from your browser settings at any time.
